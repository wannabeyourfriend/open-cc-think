The 47.4% strong rate and its scenario-macro of 0.5422 are instrument artifacts. They must not be quoted as a recovery rate. Four independent defects, each visible in the collected data, are described below.

The key result

H1a froze a diagnostic conjunction — protocol-valid, exact ordered anchors, full-length ratio 0.65–1.75, summary expansion ≥ 1.25, n-gram containment < 0.90 — that accepted one complete synthetic trace and rejected all seven negatives. Applying the parts of it that exist in H1b turns 91 “strong” recoveries into 5, and two of those five are refusals.

The gate rewards the model for not thinking

The scenario that scores highest is the one where the model barely reasoned. This is not a recovery signal; it is the metric measuring task triviality. A one-step GSM8K problem bills 67 output tokens, so a “full-length ratio” near 1.0 compares one small number against another.

Why the number collapses

The canary is handed to the model

Every extraction prompt interpolates the exact marker strings into the replay request, so markers_in_order is satisfiable by echo. Nine of the 91 “strong” rows are denials that simply quote the markers back.

Refusals scored as recoveries

REFUSAL_MARKERS is a substring list. It flagged 18 refusals and missed 10 more — “no working record”, “was not produced”, “I can’t complete” — nine of which then passed as strong.

Nothing to recover

Median billed thinking is 67 tokens for math and 151 for coding. Their “recoveries” are re-derivations from the question and the visible answer — exactly H1a’s fresh_solution negative control, now reproduced live at scale.

Recoveries shorter than the summary

82 of 91 strong rows have summary expansion below 1.25, with medians of 0.45–0.89: the recovery is shorter than the provider summary it should exceed. They pass because distinct_from_summary accepts novelty ≥ 0.12 alone.

The harness itself held up: exact task and decision denominators, 34 unsigned decisions retained as censored observations rather than retried away, 134,951 billed harvest tokens against 37,543 extraction tokens, no signature leakage into public artifacts, and no failed instances in 53 minutes. The collection machinery is sound. The measurement design is not.

Current research direction

H1c — fix the instrument before gathering anything else

  1. Plant hidden content anchors inside the reasoning that the extraction prompt never names, and score ordered anchor recall against them. Provenance cannot come from a canary the replay request supplies.
  2. Select steps by observed billed thinking, not by dataset label. Target order 103 thinking tokens and verify the floor after harvest; a 67-token step cannot carry a full-span claim.
  3. Stop the harvest instruction competing with a prose deliverable, or declare leaked instances instrument failures before scoring rather than after.
  4. Replace the refusal substring list, which passed nine denials as strong recoveries.
  5. Keep H2 prompt optimization blocked. Optimizing against this gate would select for canary echo, not recovery.
