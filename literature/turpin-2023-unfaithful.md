# Language Models Don't Always Say What They Think

- Authors: Miles Turpin, Julian Michael, Ethan Perez, Samuel R. Bowman
- Year: 2023
- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.04388

## Findings

Biasing features can change answers while generated CoT omits those influences and rationalizes the
result. Plausibility and answer consistency are therefore insufficient evidence of causal
faithfulness.

## Relevance

A fluent recovery that reaches the right answer may still be reconstructed or post hoc. This
directly motivates planted hidden anchors, wrong-prefix controls, and keeping task correctness
separate from recovery evidence.
