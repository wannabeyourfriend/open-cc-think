# Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena

- Authors: Lianmin Zheng et al.
- Year: 2023
- URL: https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/91f18a1287b398d378ef22505bf41832-Paper-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.pdf

## Findings

The work finds useful agreement between strong LLM judges and humans, while documenting position,
verbosity, self-enhancement, and limited-reasoning biases.

## Relevance

An LLM autorater can support coverage/legibility analysis only after calibration and with
order/verbosity controls. It cannot serve as the sole primary metric for extraction-prompt search.
