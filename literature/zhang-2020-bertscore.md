# BERTScore: Evaluating Text Generation with BERT

- Authors: Tianyi Zhang, Varsha Kishore, Felix Wu, Kilian Q. Weinberger, Yoav Artzi
- Year: 2020
- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.09675

## Findings

BERTScore aligns contextual token embeddings and correlates better with human text-generation
judgments than purely lexical metrics in many settings.

## Relevance

Semantic comparison may help measure stability across replay replicates. It cannot by itself
distinguish a full trace from a high-quality expansion of the provider summary, so it remains
diagnostic and cannot override protocol or control failures.
