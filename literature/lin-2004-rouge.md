# ROUGE: A Package for Automatic Evaluation of Summaries

- Author: Chin-Yew Lin
- Year: 2004
- URL: https://aclanthology.org/W04-1013/

## Findings

ROUGE provides recall-oriented n-gram and subsequence comparisons against reference summaries.

## Relevance

Surface-overlap metrics are useful copy detectors in this project. They are deliberately not
completeness or provenance metrics: a semantically equivalent paraphrase can score low, and a
verbatim summary padded with unrelated text can manipulate simple ratios.
