# Chain of Thought Monitorability: A New and Fragile Opportunity for AI Safety

- Authors: Tomek Korbak et al.
- Year: 2025
- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.11473

## Findings

Natural-language CoT can support safety monitoring, but monitorability is incomplete and may be
degraded by model development choices. The paper calls for direct measurement and preservation
rather than assuming CoT remains available or faithful.

## Relevance

The project should report coverage and legibility as useful monitorability properties while avoiding
the stronger claim that recovered text is causally faithful or verbatim.
